# One Source, Four Channels

A portfolio demo of how one verified scientific source can be adapted across channels without losing accuracy.

Live demo: https://vinaya-ai.github.io/ai-content-demo/

Topic: antibiotic stewardship and the WHO AWaRe classification.

What it shows
- Five approved claims, each linked to a public WHO or UN source
- The same claims adapted into an HCP email, a WhatsApp template (English and Hindi), an Instagram carousel and a patient note
- Channel rules and a review approach for keeping AI-assisted content accurate

Sources: WHO Bulletin (2025), WHA78/8, UN General Assembly resolution 79/2, WHO news release (29 April 2025).
Drafted with AI assistance. Every claim was checked against the sources above by the author.
